### What I actually do

Most community managers write posts. I design the systems underneath — channel architecture, permission models, onboarding and verification flows, moderation stacks, and the analytics to tell whether any of it is working. Then I run them.

Right now that's **Discord & Telegram Infrastructure Lead at Co/Unity**, building deployment templates and security layers that client communities launch from.

---

### What I build

Bots and automation, mostly for Discord:

- **Onboarding & verification pipelines** — GitHub API activity checks, on-chain verification, Airtable reporting
- **Quiz & attendance bots** — replaced manual class tracking for a security education program
- **Auto role assignment & reward distribution** — cut manual moderation workload by ~80%
- **Audit, report and application forms** — contributor workflow tooling

I'm not a software engineer by training. I build what a community needs when nobody else is going to.

---

### What I teach

I design and deliver classes on **operational blockchain security** and **social media security** at [Boring Security](https://boringsecurity.com), in both Portuguese and English.

That means staying ahead of scam and phishing techniques as they shift, and helping people limit the damage after a wallet or account gets compromised.
